Scale models for Dubai developers

In Dubai, a new project is often sold off-plan, at a launch event or in a sales centre, long before construction is finished. A detailed physical model gives buyers and brokers something real to gather around: the towers or villas, the pools and landscaping, the roads and the community around them. K&K – Kala & Kriti has built architectural models for more than 30 years, with 500+ projects delivered and 20+ international projects.

Our Dubai work includes models for Sobha and Woodland Residences by AMIS, and Dubai is one of the places where we have delivered residential society models.

What we build for Dubai projects

  • Villa and townhouse communities: individual homes, pools, landscaping, internal roads and street lighting, at a scale buyers can read at a glance.
  • Luxury towers and mixed-use developments: façade detail, podiums, amenities and retail levels, with lit floors for night views.
  • Master-plan models: large communities shown with zoning, phases, green spaces and connectivity, for launches and investor presentations.
  • Commercial and retail projects: models used for leasing pitches, investor demos and launches.

Made for launch events and sales centres

Our models are built for the places where projects are sold: sales galleries, launch events and real-estate expos. LED lighting and transparent panels bring the model to life with night-view simulation and internal illumination, so the project looks its best under event lighting.

Quality you can rely on, from drawings to delivery

Every model is custom-built by our complete in-house team of 25+ professionals, with no outsourcing, so quality and timelines stay under our control. We combine laser cutting and engraving, 3D printing, CNC milling and CAD integration with materials such as acrylic sheets, high-grade plastic and PVC, MDF and transparent panels. Finished models are crate-packed and transported with insurance, and we provide setup guidance and maintenance tips after delivery.

How a project works with us

Every model follows the same seven-step process we use for all our clients:

  • Initial consultation: understanding your project scope and vision.
  • Design inputs: gathering architectural drawings and references.
  • Quotation and proposal: a transparent cost and delivery timeline.
  • Model development: a precision build with milestone reviews.
  • Quality check: internal checks and client validation.
  • Safe delivery: crate-packed and insured transport.
  • Post-delivery support: setup guidance and maintenance tips.
