Architectural Model FAQ

Common questions from developers, architects and government teams about how we work: the process, the drawings we need, and what shapes cost and timelines.

What is an architectural scale model, and who uses one?

An architectural scale model is a physical, miniature version of a building or development, built to scale from the architect's drawings. Real-estate developers use them in sales galleries, launch events and property expos; architects use them to present designs; and government bodies use them for policy presentations, public exhibitions, investor summits and city vision launches.

What types of projects do you make models for?

We build models across all scales and sectors, including:

  • Residential societies, from small villa layouts to large integrated townships
  • Schools, colleges, universities and other educational campuses
  • IT and tech corporate parks and corporate headquarters
  • Shopping malls, retail plazas and mixed-use complexes
  • Government projects, urban planning and smart cities
  • Temples, gurudwaras and other spiritual and religious places
How does the process work?

Every project follows seven steps:

  • Initial consultation: understanding your project scope and vision.
  • Design inputs: gathering architectural drawings and references.
  • Quotation and proposal: a transparent cost and delivery timeline.
  • Model development: a precision build with milestone reviews.
  • Quality check: internal checks and client validation.
  • Safe delivery: crate-packed and insured transport.
  • Post-delivery support: setup guidance and maintenance tips.
What drawings and information do you need to start?

The more complete the inputs, the more accurate the model and the quote. Useful items include:

  • The site plan or master plan
  • Floor plans, elevations and sections of the buildings
  • Landscape plans and details of amenities
  • 3D views or renders and any colour or material references
  • The purpose of the model (sales gallery, launch event, approval meeting, exhibition), the space it will be displayed in, and your target date

If some drawings are still being finalised, send what you have and we'll tell you what else is needed.

What affects the cost of an architectural model?

Every model is custom, so we quote each project individually. The main factors are:

  • The scale and overall size of the model
  • The number of buildings and how large the site is
  • The level of detail, such as façades, balconies, interiors or sectional views
  • Lighting, for example LED-lit floors, façades and pathways
  • Landscaping, water features and other site elements
  • Materials and finishes
  • The time available before your deadline
  • Packing, transport and delivery location

You receive a transparent quotation after we review your drawings and requirements.

How long does it take to make a model?

Timelines depend on the size and complexity of the model, the level of detail and lighting, how complete the drawings are when we start, and how quickly feedback comes back at each milestone review. We confirm a delivery timeline in our proposal, so share your target date at the start and we'll plan the build around it.

How do you choose the scale?

The right scale depends on what the model needs to show and where it will be displayed. A master plan for a large township is usually built at a smaller scale so the whole site fits, while a single tower or a villa can be built larger to show more detail. We recommend a scale after looking at your drawings, the purpose of the model and the available display space.

What materials and technology do you use?

We use acrylic sheets, foam board, MDF, balsa wood, high-grade plastic and PVC, and transparent panels for illuminated layouts, with eco-friendly options on request. Our workshop uses laser cutting and engraving, 3D printing, CNC milling and CAD software integration, and LED lighting for night-view simulation and internal illumination.

Can the model be lit up?

Yes. We integrate LED lighting for internal floors, building highlights, pathways and landscape features, so the model can show how the project will look at night. This works especially well in sales galleries and at evening launch events.

Do you outsource any of the work?

No. We have a complete in-house team of 25+ professionals, which gives us full control over quality and timelines.

Which cities do you work in? Do you deliver to Dubai?

We have delivered projects in Delhi, Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Dubai and more, and have completed 20+ international projects. Models are crate-packed and transported with insurance. See our pages for Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Dubai.

Can I review the model while it is being built?

Yes. Model development includes milestone reviews, and the finished model goes through internal quality checks and client validation before delivery.

What happens after the model is delivered?

We provide setup guidance and maintenance tips so the model stays in good condition in your sales gallery or exhibition space.
