Scale models for Hyderabad's high-rise and township projects

Buyers of Hyderabad's high-rise and township projects need help picturing a home that hasn't been built yet. A physical scale model lets them stand around the project and see it all at once: the towers, the podium, the landscape, the amenities and the roads. K&K – Kala & Kriti has been turning architects' drawings into models like these for more than 30 years, with 500+ projects delivered.

In Hyderabad we have built models for Aparna Sunstone and Vasavi Ananda Nilayam. For Aparna Sunstone, the model shows the high-rise towers with lit floors, landscaped edges and the internal road network, so a buyer in the gallery can find their tower, their floor and the green spaces at a glance.

What we build for Hyderabad clients

  • High-rise residential and gated communities: façade rhythm, balcony lines, podium levels, trees and streetlights, all built to scale from the architect's drawings.
  • Integrated townships: master-plan models that show phases, zoning, open spaces and connectivity.
  • IT and tech corporate parks: Hyderabad is one of the cities where we have served corporate-park clients, with models of office towers, parking, green spaces and access-control areas.
  • Institutional and government projects: educational campuses, urban planning and smart-city models for presentations and public exhibitions.

Lighting that sells at night

Sales galleries and launch events often run into the evening. We integrate LED lighting and transparent panels so internal floors, building highlights and pathways glow the way the finished project will, which makes the model the centre of the room.
