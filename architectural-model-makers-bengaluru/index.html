Scale models for Bengaluru's builders and architects

Bengaluru buyers, investors and approval committees want to see a project before it is built. A well-made physical model shows the whole site at a glance: the towers, the landscape, the clubhouse, the roads in and out, and how everything sits together. That is what K&K – Kala & Kriti has been building for more than 30 years.

We have delivered models for Bengaluru projects including Embassy Zenith, Brigade Eternia and DNR Parklink, and Bengaluru is one of the cities where we have built both residential society models and IT and tech corporate park models.

What we build for Bengaluru projects

  • Residential societies and townships: from small villa layouts to large integrated townships, showing towers, amenities, green spaces and internal roads.
  • IT and tech corporate parks: office towers, parking zones, green spaces, utility buildings and access-control areas, with integrated LED lighting for internal floors, building highlights and pathways.
  • Schools and educational campuses: academic blocks, hostels, admin buildings, playgrounds and auditoriums, with clear functional zoning.
  • Commercial, retail and mixed-use projects: models used for leasing pitches, investor demos and launches.

Built for sales galleries, launches and expos

Most of our real-estate models go into sales galleries, launch events and property expos, where buyers walk around them and find their tower, their floor and their view. For night-view presentations we use LED lighting and transparent panels to light floors, façades and pathways. The same models also work for investor presentations and approval meetings, where a physical model explains a master plan faster than a stack of drawings.

Materials, technology and an in-house team

Our team of 25+ in-house professionals handles every model without outsourcing, which keeps quality and timelines under our control. We work with laser cutting and engraving, 3D printing, CNC milling and CAD integration, and choose materials such as acrylic sheets, foam board, MDF, balsa wood, high-grade plastic and PVC to suit each project. Eco-friendly material options are available on request.

How a project works with us

Every model follows the same seven-step process we use for all our clients:

  • Initial consultation: understanding your project scope and vision.
  • Design inputs: gathering architectural drawings and references.
  • Quotation and proposal: a transparent cost and delivery timeline.
  • Model development: a precision build with milestone reviews.
  • Quality check: internal checks and client validation.
  • Safe delivery: crate-packed and insured transport.
  • Post-delivery support: setup guidance and maintenance tips.
